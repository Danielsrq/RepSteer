Takeaway

A raw difference-of-means vector cannot steer this model into emoji-only speech: the target behavior is a coordinated multi-token byte program, and adding one direction merely promotes its marginal byte/modifier tokens until the stream collapses. This is precisely the gap ReFT/LoReFT closes — a trained low-rank rotation of the subspace can learn the coordinated emoji-generation behavior that a fixed additive vector structurally cannot. The DiM vector is highly separable (AUC≈1.0) yet not causally steerable — a clean example of "readable ≠ steerable."
