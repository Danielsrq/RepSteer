Angry LoReFT — reading with a steering intervention

Qwen3.5-4B, layer 17, rank 4. Trained (pyreft) on 24 topics of the concept corpus to turn the tone-free prompt into the angry paragraph; 6 topics held out. Two variants from the same initialisation: edits on every prompt token vs only the last one; each keeps its best held-out epoch. Then the intervention is used as a reader: V = W − R = UΣQᵀ gives read directions qᵢ (rows of Qᵀ), write directions pᵢ (columns of RᵀU) and gains σᵢ, since M = Rᵀ V = Σᵢ σᵢ pᵢ qᵢᵀ. The angry DiM vector here is recomputed on the training topics only.
