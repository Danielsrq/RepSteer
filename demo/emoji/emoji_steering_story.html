Getting a 4B model to reply in emoji: DiM attempts → LoReFT

First we tried several difference-of-means steering vectors, each swept to its best strength — most failed or were weak, and only capturing the system-prompt's conditioning offset worked at all. LoReFT then beat them cleanly. (Tab 1 shows the three that carry the story by default; the dead-end variants fold in behind a toggle.) The second tab shows why the LoReFT is trained without the system prompt: with the prompt in the training context the model already complies, so there's no gradient and the intervention learns nothing.
